The Thesis

Happiness is contagious.
So is a great story.
Both convert.

I spent eleven years running festivals that plugged consumer brands into physical moments. Not because screens don't work, but because the thing a customer remembers is the thing they pay for twice. Post-COVID, that bet got louder. The brands I've worked with (MasterCard, Smithfield, Budweiser, Jack Daniels, Weber, the NFL) all showed up for the same reason: attendance becomes affinity, affinity becomes commerce.

My job was never to throw a party. My job was to build an omnichannel marketing vehicle where one of the channels happened to be a 50,000-seat stadium parking lot on a weekend the team wasn't playing. At its core, that's promotion. Building the relationship between the consumer and the brand, then pulling it through into commerce.
